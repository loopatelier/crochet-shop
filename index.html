About the Maker

Hello, I'm Clara — the hands & heart behind every stitch.

My love affair with fiber arts began ten years ago on my grandmother's sunlit porch, learning how a simple wooden hook and a skein of wool could transform into something deeply comforting.

In a world of fast-paced mass production, Loops & Loom is my love letter to slow living. Every piece takes anywhere from six to forty hours to complete, crafted in my cozy home studio with a warm mug of chamomile tea nearby.

I exclusively source OEKO-TEX® certified organic cottons,undyed alpaca blends, and botanical-dyed merino wools so that each item feels gentle against sensitive skin and lasts for generations.
