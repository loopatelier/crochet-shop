About the Maker

Hii! I'm Ayesha - the hands & heart behind every stitch.

What started as a quiet hobby with a hook and some soft yarn has now become my favourite place to be.

Every piece I make takes hours of patience, care and love.

In a world that moves too fast, The Loop Atelier is my little slow corner. A place where handmade still means something and where every order is made with a full heart.

Thank you for being here, for supporting handmade and letting me make something special just for you.
